# CMV · Small Restaurant Owners · "Ball's in your court" (≈40s, 9:16)

**Avatar:** Restaurant / café owner (Avatar 1), small independent, Denmark.
**Core pain:** Not enough customers, paying for ads and social media that don't seem to work.
**Objective:** Meta lead ad. The CTA hands off to the Heyflow funnel.
**Format:** Cinematic seated talking-head, modelled on the Donald Miller / StoryBrand "If you run a small business…" reel.
**Length:** about 122 spoken words, so roughly 40s. A trim path to about 33s is at the bottom.

## Geo versions (recommended)

Ask CMV for active-member counts by city or postcode, then cut one version per region with the real number in row 5 and the caption:
*"[12.400] members in Aarhus"*, *"[38.000] members in København"*, and so on. The voiceover stays the same, so you only change one text layer per version. A local number will be far more persuasive than "400,000 nationwide", and it matches what the Heyflow shows after the click.

## Production notes

- **The catch:** shoot 10–15 takes. Someone off-camera drops the ball from a fixed mark above frame. Use the cleanest catch where his eyes stay down until *after* the ball is in his hand.
- **Lens and light:** low angle from table height, shallow depth of field, warm practicals behind him, dark room. Match the reference's moody contrast.
- **Captions:** one word at a time in white bold sans. Key phrases large with a warm Visa-gold glow and a small italic subline underneath (reference style).
- **The loop:** frame the toss in row 10 and the drop in row 1 from the same locked-off angle, so the end and start join seamlessly.
- **Shopfront mosaic:** use generic, unbranded shopfronts unless CMV has permission to show partner logos.
- **Safe zones:** keep captions, the poll sticker and the CTA arrow out of the top ~14% and the bottom ~35%.
- **Ad label:** "Reklame" / paid partnership from the CMV page.

## Claim checks before production

- **"One small start fee"**: the fine print must show the real figure (4.500 kr.) and that the rest is sales-based. Confirm whether the 12-month minimum should appear (expectation-setting is 9% of churn).
- **"Could bring guests back"**: keep "could". No guaranteed outcomes.
- **"Members pay… and earn cashback"**: only members earn, and only with a registered Visa card (not MobilePay). The script says "members" on purpose.
- **"You set the cashback"**: true per CMV's FAQ.
- **"Promoted in the app, newsletters and push"**: matches the partner page.
- **"Dashboard shows how many come back"**: the real dashboard shows returning-customer counts. Use the real UI with permission.
- **"Over 2,000 Danish businesses"**: matches "+2.000 af Danmarks bedste brands".
- **Receipts and poll in row 3:** illustrations of the pain, with no real numbers attached.

## Trim path to about 33s

1. Cut row 7's mini-scene line ("So next time they're hungry…") and move "a reason to pick YOU" onto row 6 as text (−3.5s).
2. Cut "Over 2,000 Danish businesses already do this." from the VO and keep **2.000+** as a text flash on row 8 (−2s).
3. Shorten row 3 to "Paid for ads and social, and nothing's really working? Right." (−1.5s).
