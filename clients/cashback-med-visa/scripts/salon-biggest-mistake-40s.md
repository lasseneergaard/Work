# CMV · Salon Owners · "The biggest mistake I made" (≈40s, 9:16)

**Avatar:** Salon / beauty / wellness owner (Avatar 2). Wants rebookings and a fuller calendar.
**Core pain:** Happy clients who still don't come back soon enough.
**Objective:** Meta lead ad. The CTA hands off to the Heyflow funnel.
**Format:** First-person owner testimonial, modelled on the Fresha "The biggest mistake I made as a salon owner…" reel.
**Length:** about 121 spoken words, so roughly 40s.

> ⚠️ **This only works with a real CMV salon partner.** "The biggest mistake I made as a salon owner" is a personal testimonial. If an actor says it, it's a fabricated testimonial, which is misleading under Danish marketing law and Meta's ad policies, and it's exactly the kind of overpromise that feeds expectation-setting churn. Every "before" line must be true for *her*. If no partner is available, use the **advisor version** at the bottom: same structure, honest framing.

## Why this version is more persuasive

1. **A confession beats a pitch.** "Before, happy clients left without rebooking…" lets the viewer recognise her own salon in someone else's mistake. That's a far stronger pull than us listing features.
2. **The before/after colour grade does the persuading visually.** Grey, grainy pain, then a colour wipe on "Now". Viewers feel the switch without being told.
3. **The pains are specific to salons**, not generic: the cut-off "shall we book your next…?", the "–30 % ONSDAG" sign, the stylist walking out with her clients following.
4. **Fresh service angles** (none of these were in earlier scripts): *"whoever does her hair"*, *"It even plans campaigns with me"* (Partner Team), *"My Instagram ads never showed me this"* (dashboard vs ads).

## Production notes

- **The catch:** a crew member drops the appointment book from a fixed mark just above frame. It's heavy enough to fall straight and land with a satisfying thump. Shoot 10+ takes.
- **Before/after grade:** grade rows 4–6 desaturated with light grain, then a clean left-to-right colour wipe at the start of row 7. Keep her look identical, so only the grade changes.
- **Captions:** reference style, 2–3 white bold words at a time, centred low-middle. The logo pops at the top only once.
- **B-roll flashes:** keep each one 1–1.5s. They're memory flashes, not scenes.
- **Safe zones:** keep the lower-third, poll sticker and CTA out of the top ~14% and the bottom ~35%.
- **Ad label:** "Reklame" / paid partnership from the CMV page.

## Claim checks before production

- **"Denmark's leading"**: **don't use** unless CMV can substantiate it. Competitors (LogBuy, PlusKort) make similar or larger reach claims. Replaced with "the Visa cashback programme 400,000 Danes use" (partner page: +400.000 members).
- **Testimonial authenticity:** see the warning at the top.
- **"Every member in my chair earns cashback"**: true for members paying with a registered Visa card. The word "member" keeps it accurate.
- **"Whoever does her hair"**: cashback is tied to the partner location. ✔
- **"Handles my marketing to members nearby"**: app, newsletters to the local area, push. ✔
- **"Plans campaigns with me"**: partner page: "we set up a meeting and make a plan" + "ongoing follow-up meetings with the Partner Team". ✔
- **Dashboard metrics:** confirm the live dashboard shows returning customers and time between visits, and get permission to show it.
- **Fine print:** 4.500 kr. start fee + sales-based. Confirm whether the 12-month minimum should appear.
- **+65 kr balance:** label it *example*.
