# CMV · Coffee Shop Owners · "Me seeing how many came back" (≈40s, 9:16)

**Avatar:** Restaurant / café owner (Avatar 1), small independent coffee shop.
**Core pain:** Started from nothing, empty afternoons, paying for ads that didn't fill the room.
**Objective:** Meta lead ad. The CTA hands off to the Heyflow funnel.
**Format:** Casual, excited couch-selfie story ("talking to a friend"), threaded with the attention tricks from the "Trending ways to start your reels" reference.
**Length:** about 122 spoken words, so roughly 40s.

> ⚠️ **This must be a real CMV café partner with her real numbers.** Her story ("opened with nothing", "almost gave up", "[3.000] kr a month on ads", "not throwing money at Instagram anymore") and every figure on screen must be true and shown from her actual partner dashboard. All numbers below are **[placeholders]**. A scripted actor with invented results would be a fake testimonial: illegal under Danish marketing law, against Meta's policies, and exactly the expectation gap behind CMV's churn.
>
> **"Her CMV app"**: partners see results in the **partner dashboard** (on the web, and fine on a phone browser), not the consumer app. That's what we screen-record.
>
> **Avatar note:** the brief opens with "target salon owners", but the hook and story are about a coffee shop. This is written for **café owners**. Salon swap lines are at the bottom.

## What we're borrowing from the reference

The reference (14s) is a montage of hook techniques, each labelled in a **handwritten font**. We use those techniques as **pattern interrupts every 4–5 seconds** inside her casual story, and label each story beat in the same handwritten style.

| Reference technique | Where it shows up in ours |
|---|---|
| **SPILL THE TEA** (leaning in, hand cupped by her mouth) | Row 2: "Okay. Coffee shop owners, you need to hear this." Labelled ***the tea ☕*** (café pun) |
| **PULL-IN REVEAL** (a hand pulls her through the frame) | Row 5: the turning point, pulled from her couch into her busy café |
| **SPECIAL DELIVERY** (an envelope on a silver tray) | Row 7: a barista slides in a **saucer with a latte and a printed receipt** showing her real number. Labelled ***the receipts 🧾*** |
| **HAND SIGNAL** (palm to the lens) | Row 10: "So I'm telling you", palm covers the lens as a transition |
| **Paper sign held up to camera** | Row 11: her handwritten CTA sign |

**The continuous attention element:** a small animated **latte cup "meter"** sits in the top corner for the whole video. It's empty while she talks about starting with nothing, wobbles during the low point, and **fills up** after she joins CMV. When it's full, it flips to her real number ("[62] regulars back ☕"). It's always moving, it mirrors her story, and viewers want to see it fill.

> Honestly? I wish someone had told me sooner. So I'm telling you.
> Check how many live near you. It's down there.

**Delivery:** not a read. Give her the beats and let her say it in her own words, in Danish. Keep the laughs, the "like", the self-interruptions. Do 3–4 loose takes and cut the best moments together.

## Hook text: your line, rewritten

Your line: *"Coffee shop owners, copy my method if you want to go from no customers to being overwhelmed with customers."*
The problem: it promises an outcome CMV can't guarantee, and "copy my method" reads like a course ad. The rewrite works through **curiosity plus a real number** instead:

- **Main (two lines):** *"me seeing how many customers came back to my café this month 😳"* → *"coffee shop owners… copy this before the café next door does 👀"*
- **Alt A:** *"I almost closed my café. This is what changed ☕️"*
- **Alt B:** *"POV: you check your numbers and [62] customers came back this month"*
- **Alt C:** *"coffee shop owners, nobody told me this when I opened 😭"*

## Production notes

- **Authenticity is the whole ad.** Real partner, her real couch, her real café, her own phone. Use her *actual* old footage for the memory flashes if she has it.
- **Filming:** row 1 from a phone propped on the coffee table; everything else selfie in her hand; row 7 top-down; row 8 a real screen recording of her dashboard.
- **Pull-in reveal (row 5):** shoot both halves with the camera at the same height and the same arm position, so the pull reads as one continuous move.
- **Labels:** handwritten font in white, matching the reference (*the tea ☕, the backstory, the low point, the thing that changed, the receipts 🧾*).
- **Latte meter:** small (about 12% of the frame width), top-right, inside the safe zone. Animate it continuously, even a gentle wobble while it's "waiting".
- **Safe zones:** keep labels, the meter and the CTA out of the top ~14% and the bottom ~35%.
- **Ad label:** "Reklame" / paid partnership from the CMV page.

## Claim checks before production

- **Every personal statement must be true for her:** opened with nothing, almost gave up, the monthly ad spend, no longer relying on Instagram ads.
- **[62] returning customers / [18.400] kr**: must come from her own partner dashboard for the stated month. Show the dashboard on screen as proof.
- **"What those members spent here"**: worded on purpose. Dashboard turnover is spend by CMV members. **Don't** call it "money CMV made me" or "extra sales"; incrementality is CMV's #1 churn objection.
- **"Their members pay here with their Visa, get a bit of cashback"**: true for members with a registered Visa card. ✔
- **"They show my café to their 400,000 members"**: CMV markets partners in the app, on the web, in newsletters and by push. ✔ (Not "organic". It's CMV's own channels.)
- **"I'm not throwing money at Instagram anymore"**: her choice, and only if true. Don't imply CMV *replaces* all marketing for everyone.
- **Fine print:** 4.500 kr. start fee + sales-based. Confirm whether the 12-month minimum should appear.
