# CMV · Hair Salon Owners · "She never booked the next one" (40s, 9:16)

**Avatar:** Local service owner, hair salon (Avatar 2).
**Core pain:** Happy clients who don't come back soon enough.
**Objective:** Meta lead ad. The CTA hands off to the Heyflow funnel.
**Format:** UGC creator talking-head, modelled on the "If I owned a hair salon" reference reel.
**Length:** about 128 spoken words, which is roughly 40s with tight jump cuts. A trim path is at the bottom.

## What we're borrowing from the reference (and what we're changing)

| Reference reel | Our version |

| Creator in a white tee holding a fluffy lav mic up to her mouth. Advisor voice ("here's exactly how I'd market it") | Same look, same advisor voice, same mic prop |

## Hook variants to test against the main hook

All three keep rows 2–10 unchanged. Swap only the first 4 seconds.

1. **Cold open on the pain:** Starts on the full-screen empty chair. VO: *"She loved her hair. So why is her next appointment still empty?"*
2. **Swiss cheese first:** Starts on the calendar with holes popping. VO: *"Salon owners, if your Tuesday looks like this, keep watching."*
3. **The number:** Starts on a close-up. VO: *"If your clients come back every 10 weeks instead of every 6, this is for you."*

**Organic / comment version of the CTA** (closer to the reference): *"Comment SALON and I'll send you how it works for salons."* Only use it if someone is set up to answer DMs fast; median first contact is currently about 35 hours.

## Production notes

- **Presenter:** a woman aged 28–40 who looks at home in a salon. If a real salon owner can deliver it, change "here's exactly what I'd do" to "here's what I do". White tee, jeans, and the fluffy lav mic kept visibly in hand, because that's the reference's signature.
- **Location:** a real salon with warm natural light, mirrors, styling chairs and a reception desk. No office.
- **Captions:** one word at a time, white bold sans. Key words (*salon, exactly, happy, Tuesday, zero, cashback, automatically, prices, dashboard, near*) in italic serif, CMV light blue.
- **Safe zones (Reels/Stories):** keep all text and the CTA arrow out of the top ~14% and the bottom ~35% of the frame.
- **Brand:** no logo until the end card. Inside the app and dashboard screens the brand is visible naturally, so the ad stays native and doesn't read as an ad until the payoff.
- **Pacing:** remove every breath and pause *except* the deliberate silent beat on "But" in row 3.
- **Ad label:** run from the CMV page as a paid partnership / "Reklame" per Danish marketing rules.

## Claim checks before production

- **400,000+**: matches the partner page (approved wording "+400.000 medlemmer").
- **"Get cashback automatically"**: true for registered Visa cards. Avoid "every client" or "always" (MobilePay doesn't earn).
- **"You choose the cashback rate"**: true per the FAQ. The 750 kr. / 10 % example must stay labelled *Example*.
- **Dashboard**: use the real partner dashboard UI with permission. Show metric *labels*, not invented results. It shows aggregate counts, so say "how many came back", not "who".
- **"6 → 10 weeks"**: illustrative framing of the pain, not a CMV statistic.
- **Price fine print**: confirm the exact wording with the client, and whether the 12-month minimum should appear. Expectation-setting is 9% of documented churn.
- **CTA**: the first Heyflow screen must deliver on "see how many members near you". The first step currently loses 88%, so the promise and the first screen have to match.

## Trim path if it runs long (target 38–40s)

1. Cut "You get shown to them in the app, newsletters and push." and let the row 8 screen recording carry it silently with the chips (−3s).
2. Shorten the hook to "Salon owners, here's exactly what I'd do." (−1.5s). Keep this as a separate test so the main hook stays as briefed.
