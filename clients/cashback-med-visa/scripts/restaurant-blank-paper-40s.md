# CMV · Restaurant & Café Owners · "What's on this paper?" (≈40s, 9:16)

**Avatar:** Restaurant / café owner (Avatar 1). Wants fuller quiet periods and more repeat visits.
**Core pain:** Empty tables on weekdays, guests who visit once, always needing new customers.
**Objective:** Meta lead ad. The CTA hands off to the Heyflow funnel.
**Format:** Seated creator talking-head with a prop hook, modelled on the WAWCD "Hey restaurant owners" reel.
**Length:** about 120 spoken words + a 2s end card, so 38–40s. A trim path to about 33s is at the bottom.

---

## What we're borrowing from the reference (and what we're changing)

| Reference reel (WAWCD) | Our version |
|---|---|
| "Hey restaurant owners" + an empty mug pushed into the lens and flipped. "Nothing. Just like your database." | "Hey restaurant owners" + a **blank sheet of paper** pushed into the lens and flipped. "Nothing. Just like your booking list on a Tuesday." |
| Starts upright in an armchair | Starts **upside down on a restaurant booth sofa**, hair hanging to the floor, then a **paper-wipe match cut** puts her upright |
| Pain stated as a rule: "The rule is very simple: reply late, they move to another brand." | Same rhythm: "The rule is simple: give people a reason to come back, or they'll try the place next door." |
| "It's not magic, trust me. That's exactly what WAWCD does for your business." | The product arrives naturally after the rule, with no hard pitch |
| Mostly talking head, a phone prop, kinetic text | Talking head **plus** split screens, PiP B-roll, a map, a graph, an app screen recording and a dashboard. Something new every 2–3s |
| Mint one-word pill captions + big 3D headline words + logo watermark top-left | Same system in CMV colours: **Visa-blue pill captions**, white/light-blue 3D headlines, small **Cashback \| VISA** watermark |
| End card: logo + "Visit now" | End card: logo + "Bliv partner" + honest price line |

**Set:** shoot inside a real restaurant on an empty weekday afternoon. Use a booth/banquette sofa, with laid tables and nobody sitting at them behind her. The empty room *is* the pain, so it's in every frame. Warm, cosy light like the reference (practical lamps, candles on the tables).
**Wardrobe:** relaxed creator look like the reference (oversized shirt over a dark top), lav mic clipped and visible.

## Production notes

- **Upside-down shot:** lock the camera off and **don't move it** between rows 1–4. The paper-wipe match cut only works if the frame is identical when she's upright. Shoot the upright take straight after, paper in the same spot.
- **Hair:** brush it out before the take so it falls cleanly. Light from slightly below so her face isn't in shadow while she's inverted. Keep the inverted section short (under 5s) for her comfort and so the delivery stays natural.
- **Captions:** one word at a time in Visa-blue pills (white text). Key beats as big 3D headlines: *HEY RESTAURANT OWNERS, NOTHING, TUESDAY, THE RULE, 400.000+, Bliv partner*.
- **Split-screen footage:** if you can, film the Saturday and Tuesday shots in the same restaurant from the same angle. Same room, different day is the whole point.
- **Safe zones (Reels/Stories):** keep text, the poll sticker and the CTA arrow out of the top ~14% and the bottom ~35% of the frame.
- **Pacing:** cut out every breath. The two intentional silences are the bass hit on "Nothing" and the crickets.
- **Ad label:** run from the CMV page as a paid partnership / "Reklame".

## Claim checks before production

- **400,000+**: matches the partner page wording.
- **"Get cashback automatically"**: true for registered Visa cards. Don't say "every guest" (only members earn, and not via MobilePay).
- **"Members near you"**: newsletters are described on the partner page as "always targeted at new customers in your local area", and targeting uses postcode. OK.
- **Dashboard:** use the real UI with permission. It shows aggregate counts, so say "how many came back", not "who".
- **450 kr. / 10 %**: labelled *Example*. Partners set their own rate.
- **"94 days ago" and the promo graph:** illustrations of the pain, not CMV data.
- **Push notification copy:** no "today only" or timed-offer promises unless CMV confirms partners can run them.
- **Price fine print:** confirm the exact wording and whether the 12-month minimum should appear.
- **CTA:** the first Heyflow screen must deliver "see how many members live near you". The first step currently loses 88%.
