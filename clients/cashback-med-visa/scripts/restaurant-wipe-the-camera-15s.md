## Production notes

- **Lock the camera off** between the empty and full takes, with identical framing and focus. The wipe only works if the reveal is the exact same shot.
- **Lighting:** keep the empty take slightly cooler and dimmer and the full take warmer. Change the light, not the angle.
- **Timestamp:** add it in post so it's pixel-identical in both shots. That's what makes "same Tuesday, full table" read instantly.
- **Captions:** the first two lines stay small and casual like the reference (that's what makes the gag feel native). Switch to bolder text from the hook onwards.
- **Card terminal shot:** in Denmark the server usually brings the terminal to the table. Shoot it that way so it feels real.
- **Safe zones:** keep text and the logo out of the top ~14% and the bottom ~35%. The reference's bottom-centre logo would sit under the Reels caption, so move it up.

## Claim checks

- **"Get more customers"**: CMV's own promise on the partner page ("vi skaffer flere kunder til din butik"). Keep it as the hook, but don't add numbers or "guaranteed".
- **Empty → full Tuesday:** a dramatisation. Don't add text claiming CMV filled a specific restaurant.
- **"Get cashback automatically"**: true for registered Visa cards. The ad says "they", not "every guest".
- **400,000+**: matches the partner page.
- **Push notification copy:** no "today only" or timed offers unless CMV confirms partners can run them.
- **Price fine print:** confirm the wording and whether the 12-month minimum should appear.
- **CTA:** must match the first Heyflow screen ("how many members near you").
