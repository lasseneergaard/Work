# CMV · Salon Owners · "The 30-day plan" (≈39s, 9:16)

**Avatar:** Salon / beauty / wellness owner (Avatar 2). Wants rebookings and a fuller calendar.
**Core pain:** Happy clients who still don't come back soon enough.
**Objective:** Meta lead ad. The CTA hands off to the Heyflow funnel.
**Format:** Salon-coach walk-and-talk numbered list, modelled on the "If you need more clients in your salon right now, here is exactly what I would do…" reel.
**Length:** about 118 spoken words, so roughly 39s.

> **Hook change:** the brief's hook promises *"fully booked in less than 30 days"*. CMV can't substantiate a booking outcome, and promising one is exactly the expectation gap behind 9% of documented churn. The script keeps the 30-day urgency as **a plan of actions** ("here's exactly what I'd do in the next 30 days"), not a guaranteed result.

## Full voiceover (read-through)

> So if you need more clients for your salon, here's exactly what I'd do in the next 30 days.
> One: stop chasing strangers. I'd join Cashback med Visa, so members earn cashback every time they're in my chair.
> Two: kill the Wednesday discount. It trains clients to wait for a sale. Cashback keeps my prices full.
> Three: make the loyalty belong to the salon. The cashback's earned here, not with one stylist.
> Four: get in front of their 400,000 members, starting with the ones a few streets away.
> Five: stop paying for clicks I can't follow. It's sales-based, and I see how many clients come back.
> Tap below and see how many members live around your salon.

## Why this version is more persuasive (and different from the earlier scripts)

1. **Every step starts with "stop" or "kill".** Loss framing hits harder than benefit framing. The owner hears five things she's doing that cost her money, and each one is fixed by a piece of CMV.
2. **A new insight we hadn't used:** *"It trains clients to wait for a sale."* That explains *why* discounts hurt (behaviour), not just that they "look cheap".
3. **New service lines:**
   - "Stop chasing strangers" (work existing clients first)
   - "make the loyalty belong to the salon"
   - "starting with the ones a few streets away" (local targeting, mirroring the reference's radius tip)
   - "stop paying for clicks I can't follow" + "sales-based"
4. **The Notes-app checklist is the retention engine.** Viewers see 5 empty boxes at second 4 and want to watch them all get ticked. The pink "Save this plan 📌" sticker drives saves, which feed the algorithm.

## Production notes

- **Walk-in:** gimbal backing up in front of him from the far end of the salon, landing at medium close-up on the first word. Rehearse so the towel toss lands mid-stride.
- **Towel catch:** a stylist tosses from a fixed spot off-screen left. Shoot 10+ takes and keep one where his eyes stay on the lens.
- **Notes card:** design it to look exactly like iPhone Notes (yellow header, checkboxes). It should feel like his real phone, not an ad graphic.
- **Captions:** reference style, small white serif sentences low-centre. The big numerals **1–5** pop beside his raised hand.
- **–30 % sign prop:** print it to look genuinely salon-made (handwritten marker on A4) so the rip feels real.
- **Safe zones:** keep the Notes card, the pink sticker and the CTA out of the top ~14% and the bottom ~35%.
- **Ad label:** "Reklame" / paid partnership from the CMV page. He speaks as a salon coach ("here's what I'd do"), so no false testimonial claim.
