# CMV · Hair & Beauty Salon Owners · "Here's what you'd get" (≈40s, 9:16)

**Avatar:** Salon / beauty / wellness owner (Avatar 2). Wants rebookings and a fuller calendar.
**Core pain:** Happy clients who still don't come back soon enough.
**Objective:** Meta lead ad. The CTA hands off to the Heyflow funnel.
**Format:** Three-chapter creator explainer, modelled on the "If your salon or clinic hired us to manage your socials…" reel.
**Length:** about 123 spoken words, so 40–41s. A trim path is at the bottom.

> **Wording note:** in CMV language, *members* are the consumers and businesses are *partners*. The script says "joined Cashback med Visa" so a salon owner never hears themselves called a "member".

## Full voiceover (read-through)

> If you own a hair or beauty salon that's not busy enough, and you joined Cashback med Visa tomorrow… here's what you'd get.
> Your clients love your work. They just don't come back fast enough.
> One: the client who drifts for twelve weeks. Every visit earns her cashback at your salon. Not your stylist's. Yours. And your prices stay full, so you never look cheap.
> Two: the empty Wednesday. You're shown to 400,000 members, picked by where they live and what they spend. And for four weeks, you're the featured new salon.
> Three: ad money you can't track. Here, you see how many came back, and how long they waited.
> Tap below and see how many members live near your salon.

## Why this one hits harder (vs. the earlier scripts)

1. **Every service now arrives as the answer to a named pain.** The chapter titles are the problems the owner lives with: *the client who drifts, the empty Wednesday, the ad money you can't track*.
2. **New proof points we hadn't used yet:** cashback is tied to **the salon, not the stylist** · **full prices** instead of "no stamp cards" · targeting by **area and spending** · **four weeks featured as a new partner** · **days between visits** · **sales-based, no fixed monthly fee** · the **Partner Team** sets it up.
3. **Loss aversion, honestly done.** "I'll book next time 💕" → *Seen* → silence, plus the 5.600 kr example card, makes the cost of doing nothing feel real without any made-up CMV result.
4. **The stylist line goes straight at a fear owners rarely say out loud:** if a stylist leaves, her clients often follow. "Not your stylist's. Yours."

## Production notes

- **The catch:** a crew member drops the mic from a fixed mark just above frame. Shoot 10+ takes and keep the one where her eyes never leave the lens.
- **Look:** cool grey wall for setup A, a bouclé armchair with soft daylight and curtains for setup B, cream chapter cards. Keep it calm and premium like the reference. The energy comes from the edit, not loud colours.
- **Captions:** small lowercase sans, bold for key words, *italic serif* for emphasis (*hair or beauty salon, drifts, yours, wednesday, track*).
- **Chapter cards:** keep them to 1.5–2s each. They're breathers, not pauses.
- **Safe zones:** keep pills, bubbles and the CTA out of the top ~14% and the bottom ~35%.
- **Ad label:** "Reklame" / paid partnership from the CMV page.

## Claim checks before production

- **"Every visit earns her cashback"**: true when she's a member paying with a registered Visa card. The on-screen line says how.
- **"At your salon. Not your stylist's."**: cashback is tied to the partner location, not to an individual employee. ✔
- **"Prices stay full"**: the price list doesn't change. Cashback is paid back afterwards. ✔
- **"Picked by where they live and what they spend"**: the partner page says targeting is "based on consumption data and demographics". **Confirm with CMV** that a salon can actually target by spending category before using "what they spend".
- **"Four weeks featured as a new partner"**: ✔ ("Fremhævet som ny partner i fire uger").
- **Dashboard "how many came back / days between visits"**: matches the dashboard shown on the partner page. Confirm the live dashboard has both metrics and get permission to show it.
- **"Sales-based, no fixed monthly fee"**: ✔ ("salgsbaseret uden faste omkostninger"). The fine print must still show the 4.500 kr. start fee, and confirm whether the 12-month minimum should appear.
- **5.600 kr card and +70 kr app balance**: label both *example*.

## Trim path (if the read runs past 40s)

1. Cut "And for four weeks, you're the featured new salon" from the VO and leave it as a pill (−2.5s).
2. Shorten the hook to "Own a hair or beauty salon that's not busy enough? Here's what Cashback med Visa gives you." (−1.5s).
