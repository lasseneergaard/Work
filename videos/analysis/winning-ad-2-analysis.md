### 2. Qualitative Creative Framework Audit (Step-by-Step)

#### Step 1: Scroll Stopper (00:00–00:01) — Score: 8.5/10
- **Visual:** The presenter shoves the large, branded Cellsius pillow directly into the camera lens, then snaps back to reveal his face and torso.
- **Why it works:** Physical lens intrusion creates immediate movement and an instant 3D depth shift in the feed.
- **Gut Reaction:** *"What is this white triangular cushion being shoved into my face?"*

#### Step 2: Hook (00:01–00:06) — Score: 8.0/10
- **Audio/Visual Line:** *« Tu dors sur le côté ? Il te faut ça ! Tu te réveilles avec une douleur à la hanche, au bas du dos, ou une gêne liée à la sciatique ? Le problème ne vient peut-être pas de ton matelas. »* ("Do you sleep on your side? You need this! Do you wake up with hip pain, lower back pain or sciatica discomfort? The problem might not be your mattress.")
- **Screening:** Direct binary filter: *« Tu dors sur le côté ? »* (screens 70%+ of the sleeping population).
- **The "One Belief":** Your morning lumbar and sciatic pain is not an inevitable mystery or a bad mattress — it is an unaligned sleeping mechanic.
- **Why it works:** Rapid symptom stacking (hip → lower back → sciatica) paired with hands touching the lower back, ending with a belief-shattering rock thrown at the traditional enemy (*« pas de ton matelas »*).

#### Step 3: Hold & Tactile UMP/UMS (00:07–00:18) — Score: 9.5/10
- **The "Finger-Leg" Analogy (00:08–00:14):** Instead of costly 3D CGI, the creator uses two fingers on his right hand as the legs and his left hand as the pelvis:
  - Top finger drops forward → Pelvis tilts → Spine twists out of alignment.
- **Why it converts:** Extreme cognitive simplicity. Any viewer understands the pelvic drop in 4 seconds flat. Bypasses medical jargon and feels immediately relatable.
- **UMS Overlay (00:15–00:18):** Split-screen graphic of a woman sleeping with vs. without the pillow, highlighting the straight green spine vector.

#### Step 4: Body Narrative & Feature Stack (00:19–00:37) — Score: 9.0/10

| Framework Component | Execution in the Ad | Persuasion Lever |
|---|---|---|
| Benefits First (00:19–00:22) | 3-finger tally: 1) more comfortable nights, 2) fewer wake-ups, 3) waking up fully rested. | Encourages Dreams: the outcome is sold before the features. |
| Physical Demo & Tactile Proof (00:23–00:30) | Squeezes the foam with two hands (dense memory rebound), pulls the strap (anti-slip security), flashes the Oeko-Tex Standard 100 certificate on a smartphone. | Addresses Skepticism: tangible, visible proof of quality. |
| Overcoming Friction & Lazy Objections (00:31–00:37) | « Pas de médicament » (holds green pills) · « Pas d'installation compliquée » · « Pas besoin de replacer un oreiller mal adapté toute la nuit » (wrestles with an unruly bed pillow). | Throws Rocks at Enemies: pills and regular pillows are positioned as the old, failed way. |

#### Step 5: Social Proof & Authority Stacking (00:38–00:44) — Score: 9.5/10
- **Proof Stack:**
  - *« Designé en Suisse »* (European precision & medical quality association).
  - Physical smartphone screen: **+9,400 verified reviews, 4.2/5 on Trustpilot, 96% user satisfaction.**
- **Why it works:** Showing the raw Trustpilot mobile screen directly to the camera bypasses ad skepticism. It feels like verified third-party reality.

#### Step 6: CTA & Scarcity (00:45–00:52) — Score: 8.5/10
- **Urgency:** *« La demande explose en ce moment, et les stocks partent très vite. »* ("Demand is exploding right now, and stock is going fast.")
- **CTA:** *« Clique tout de suite juste en dessous pour vérifier s'il en reste encore un pour toi, ou quelqu'un de ta famille qui en a vraiment besoin. »* ("Click right below to check if there's still one left for you, or for someone in your family who really needs it.")
- **Emotional Angle:** Soft referral trigger (*« ou quelqu'un de ta famille »*), widening buyer intent to gift-giving or helping an aging parent.

## Part 2: Diagnosis — Why This Video Hits Spend Ceilings

- **Direct-Product Hook Vulnerability:** Starting with *« Ça, c'est le coussin genoux Cellsius »* in the first second immediately flags the video as a commercial pitch. Cold audiences with high ad blindness swipe away before reaching the finger demonstration (00:08).
- **Avatar Split (Tu vs. Vous):** The casual, fast-talking tutoiement works for the 35–50 demographic, but alienates 60+ seniors who respond better to professional authority and vouvoiement.
- **Missing AOV Engine:** Unlike Ad #1, this ad does not pitch the "Pack de 2 + 2 Housses Offertes" bundle. It only sells a single unit (*« s'il en reste encore un pour toi »*). This limits AOV, compresses margin and makes it harder for Meta to bid aggressively at higher scale.
